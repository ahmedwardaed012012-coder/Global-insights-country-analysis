# 🌍 Global Insights — Country Development & Economic Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze global economic performance and country development indicators, including GDP, population, international trade, inflation, economic growth, public health, technology, and electricity.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive view of global development by transforming country-level data into interactive visualizations and analytical insights.

The dashboard enables users to compare countries, explore economic indicators, identify leading economies, and investigate relationships between economic performance, technology, and public health.

The project is organized into four main analytical and introductory pages, followed by a closing page:

- **Welcome Page**
- **Executive Overview**
- **Economic Analysis**
- **Trends & Deep Insights**
- **Thank You Page**

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level overview of global economic performance and country development.

Key visualizations include:

- Total Countries & Territories
- Total Population
- Total GDP
- Average GDP Growth
- Top 10 Economies by GDP
- Global Population Distribution
- GDP per Capita vs. Life Expectancy
- Country Performance Matrix

### 2. Economic Analysis

Focuses on international trade and economic performance across countries.

Key visualizations include:

- Total Trade Value
- Net Trade Balance
- Exports vs. Imports by Country
- Inflation Rate vs. GDP Growth
- Top 10 Countries by Trade Balance
- Top 10 Countries by GDP per Capita
- Interactive GDP Growth and Inflation Filters

### 3. Trends & Deep Insights

Explores relationships between economic development, infrastructure, technology, and public health.

Key visualizations include:

- Electricity Production vs. Consumption
- Top 10 Countries by Life Expectancy
- Infant Mortality vs. Life Expectancy
- Internet Usage vs. GDP per Capita
- Highest GDP per Capita
- Average Life Expectancy
- Interactive Development Indicator Filters

### 4. Welcome & Thank You Pages

The dashboard also includes a dedicated welcome page introducing the project and a closing page that completes the dashboard experience.

## 💡 Key Analytical Questions

This dashboard helps explore important questions about global development:

- Which countries have the largest economies by GDP?
- How does GDP per capita relate to life expectancy?
- Which countries have the strongest trade balances?
- How do inflation rates vary across countries with different GDP growth rates?
- What relationship exists between infant mortality and life expectancy?
- How does internet usage vary across countries with different GDP per capita?
- Which countries lead in life expectancy?
- How do electricity production and consumption compare across countries?

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objective of this project is to transform country-level economic and development data into an interactive analytical experience that helps users:

- Compare economic performance across countries.
- Explore international trade patterns.
- Investigate economic growth and inflation.
- Analyze health and technology indicators.
- Identify differences in development levels.
- Discover relationships between economic and social indicators.

## 📊 Dashboard Structure

The project follows a four-part analytical structure, supported by introductory and closing pages.

**Page 1 — Welcome**  
Introduces the Global Insights project and its purpose.

**Page 2 — Executive Overview**  
Provides a high-level overview of global economic performance, population, GDP, and development indicators.

**Page 3 — Economic Analysis**  
Examines international trade, trade balances, inflation, and economic growth.

**Page 4 — Trends & Deep Insights**  
Explores relationships between public health, technology, electricity, and economic development.

**Page 5 — Thank You**  
Provides a closing page for the dashboard.
